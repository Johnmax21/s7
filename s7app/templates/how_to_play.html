HOW TO PLAY

Four moves.
One winner.

Every match comes down to the same loop — pick a card, see who wins the round, and spend your Boost and Support at the right moment.

1

Pick a card

Tap any unused card from your deck. Your opponent picks at the same time — neither of you sees the other's choice until both are locked in.

3

Use your Boost

One +10 boost, once per match. Activate it before you play a card — it applies to whichever stat that card uses.

bolt

+10 to your stat

Used once — gone for the rest of the match

info

If your card's own ability would already fire this round, Boost quietly holds itself back — don't waste it stacking on a bonus you were getting anyway.

4

Play a Support card

One Support card per match, active for 3 rounds. Pick the one that matches your plan.
